# Final owner actions

Only the app owner can complete these external steps:

1. Create and activate `premium_annual` with base plan `annual-auto`, yearly auto-renewal and USD 2.99 base price; create no monthly plan or Play free-trial offer.
2. Enter the live Privacy, Support and Subscription Terms URLs in Play Console (they are already wired into the app).
3. Create or restore the upload key interactively, keep it outside Git, back it up securely, and configure ignored `android/keystore.properties` without sharing passwords.
4. Upload the signed AAB to Internal testing; add license testers and test Billing only through the Play testing install link.
5. Complete Data Safety, Ads, App access, exact-alarm, full-screen intent, foreground-service, target audience and content-rating declarations.
6. Upload approved screenshots/feature graphic/listing copy and confirm countries, taxes and regional prices.
7. Listen to ringtone, TTS and recording alarms on the Samsung and confirm Stop/Dismiss behavior before production submission.
