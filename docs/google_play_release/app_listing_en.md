# Store listing — English

## App name

Smart Voice Alarm

## Short description

Build alarms with ringtones, device speech and your own recordings.

## Full description

Smart Voice Alarm lets you create reliable alarms with repeat schedules, bundled ringtones, on-device text-to-speech and audio clips you record yourself. Arrange spoken text and recordings into a sequence for a more personal wake-up routine.

Key features:

- Exact alarms with weekday schedules.
- Bundled ringtones, device TTS and local recordings.
- Full-screen alarm experience with clear stop controls.
- Voice selection based on capabilities exposed by the device.
- Light/dark themes and 13 locales.
- No ads, analytics or account registration.

Some Android permissions are required for reliable alarms. Microphone access is requested only when you choose to record an alarm clip.
