Smart Voice Alarm

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 4, 2026

Smart Voice Alarm is a local-first alarm application developed by Nguyên Đức. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Data stored on your device

Alarm schedules and labels, repeat settings, theme and language, selected system voice, permission-related settings, and audio sequences are stored in the app's private storage. The developer does not operate a server that receives this data.

Microphone and recorded alarm audio

The app requests microphone access only after you choose to start recording and see an explanation. Recording never starts automatically or in the background. Audio clips are stored in app-specific local storage and are not uploaded, shared, attached to support email, or sent to analytics by app code. On Android, the recording directory is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. You can remove a clip by deleting/replacing its recording or deleting the last alarm that references it; uninstalling or clearing app data also removes local files.

Android alarm permissions

Notification access is used for alarms and optional reminders. Exact-alarm access schedules the core wake-up function. Full-screen intent can show the ringing screen for an alarm. A media-playback foreground service, wake lock, vibration and boot/time-change receiver help an active or rebooted alarm work reliably. The foreground service does not record audio.

System voices and network

Text-to-speech uses voices exposed by the device's TTS engine. A voice marked as requiring network access may contact its engine provider under that provider's terms. The app can open external HTTPS legal/support pages and a user-initiated support email. It contains no developer analytics, advertising, Firebase or crash-reporting SDK.

Purchases

The current Android release is distributed as a paid app; purchase and payment data are handled by Google Play. The app does not initialize an in-app purchase flow in this release.

Support

When you choose Contact Support, the app prepares an email containing the app version/build and platform. It does not include alarm labels, recordings, passwords or a device identifier. Any additional information you type is sent through your email provider and used only to answer the request.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it, clear app data or uninstall. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to respond, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations; request deletion by contacting the address below.

Children

The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. Do not include a child's personal information in support email.

Security and changes

Private app storage and platform permission controls protect local data, but no system is completely secure. Material policy changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
