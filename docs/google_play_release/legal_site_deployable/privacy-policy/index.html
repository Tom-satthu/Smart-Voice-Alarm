Smart Voice Alarm

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 4, 2026

Smart Voice Alarm is a local-first alarm application developed by Nguyên Đức.

Local app and trial data

Alarm schedules, labels, voice sequences, preferences and the app-managed seven-day trial state are stored in app-private local storage. Trial state includes UTC timestamps, permanent-expiry state and a short subscription-entitlement cache. The app does not use IMEI, Advertising ID, device fingerprinting, an account or a developer backend. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local trial data and may allow a new local trial.

Recordings and device voices

Microphone access is requested only after you choose to record. Recordings remain in app-specific storage, are excluded from Android backup/device transfer, and are not uploaded or sent to analytics by app code. System text-to-speech voices are provided by the selected device engine; network-required voices may contact that provider under its policy.

Subscription and Google Play

The app is free to download. After the seven-day app-managed trial, ordinary app use requires the yearly auto-renewing Premium subscription. Google Play Billing processes payment and card details. The app receives product metadata, purchase state and an opaque purchase token needed for client-side entitlement checks; it does not receive card numbers, and it does not log or share purchase tokens. Subscriptions are managed or canceled in Google Play.

Other services

The app contains no developer analytics, advertising, Firebase or crash-reporting SDK and no custom HTTP client/backend for app content. External legal pages and support email open only after a user action.

Permissions

Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen intent, media-playback foreground service, wake lock, vibration and reboot/time-change handling support reliable alarms. The foreground service does not record audio.

Support and deletion

Support email can include app/build/platform diagnostics but does not automatically include alarm text, recordings, purchase tokens or device identifiers. Local data remains until removed in app, cleared or uninstalled. Google Play retains subscription records under Google's policies. Ask for support-email deletion at the address below.

Children, security and changes

The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. Platform storage and permission controls are used, but no system is completely secure. Material changes will be posted here with a revised date.
