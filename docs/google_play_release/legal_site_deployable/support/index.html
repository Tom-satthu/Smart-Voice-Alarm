Permission help

  • Microphone: required only when you choose to record. If blocked, open Android Settings → Apps → Smart Voice Alarm → Permissions.
  • Notifications: allow alarm notifications so scheduled alarms remain visible and actionable.
  • Exact alarms/full-screen access: keep the alarm-related special access enabled for reliable time-critical alerts.

Response times are not guaranteed. For urgent safety needs, use your device's built-in alarm as a backup.
