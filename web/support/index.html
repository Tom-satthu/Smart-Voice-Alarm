Common topics

  • Free limit: Free includes up to 3 alarms. Unlock Unlimited Alarms with Premium Lifetime.
  • Android exact alarms: Allow exact alarms / alarms & reminders for reliable wake-ups.
  • iOS when the app is killed: Custom voice playback cannot continue after iOS fully terminates the process; a local notification with system sound is used instead.
  • More voices: Download system voices from Android TTS settings or iOS Spoken Content settings, then refresh in the App.
  • Restore purchase: Use Restore Purchase on the Premium screen after reinstalling.
