Smart Voice Alarm

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Smart Voice Alarm (“the App”) is a local-first mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and what it does not.

Summary

  • Alarm data, recordings, and settings are stored on your device.
  • There are no user accounts and no cloud sync.
  • We do not operate an application server that receives your alarm content.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

Depending on how you use the App, the following may be stored locally:

  • Alarm schedules, labels, and preferences
  • Voice sequences and text used for on-device text-to-speech
  • Audio recordings you create inside the App
  • Theme, language, reminder, and preferred voice settings

This information stays on your device unless you choose to back up your device through Apple, Google, or another backup service you control.

No accounts and no cloud service

The App does not create accounts, does not require sign-in, and does not sync alarms or recordings to a Smart Voice Alarm server.

Microphone

Microphone access is requested only so you can record voice segments when you choose to. Recordings are saved locally. The App does not upload recording audio to a Smart Voice Alarm server.

Text-to-speech

Spoken text uses the device’s system text-to-speech engine and installed system voices. The App does not send TTS content to a Smart Voice Alarm backend.

Notifications and alarms

Local notifications and platform alarm APIs are used to wake or alert you at times you schedule. These are processed by the operating system on your device.

In-app purchases

The App is distributed as a paid download through Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment details and purchase records are handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies; the App does not collect or store payment-card information.

Analytics, advertising, and sale of data

The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not sell your personal data.

Children

The App is a general productivity tool. It is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit personal information about children through support channels.

Your choices

You can delete alarms, recordings, and app data from the device, revoke microphone or notification permissions in system settings, and uninstall the App at any time.
